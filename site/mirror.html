iPod Mirror
OverviewSourceDownload

Your iPod. On your Mac.

A little screen with a bigger home. Tap, swipe and type from your Mac.

A familiar face.

The original photographic iPod body and classic iOS Home screen, paired with a polished mirror and reflection. A thin frame keeps the screen in focus.

A slim, always-visible glass bar holds Home, Lock, screenshots, audio and settings without crowding your apps.

Plug in. Bring it over.

Connect your jailbroken iPod by USB, enable Veency, and choose the device in Connection settings. Accept its connection request, then click to tap, drag to swipe, or use your Mac keyboard.

Hear it here, too.

Optional device helpers bring iPod audio to your Mac. An experimental direct screen stream is available at 320 × 480; Veency still handles input. Audio and video are not synchronized, and the iPod speaker stays on.

Helper setup instructions ›

Made for a classic.

Tested on iPod touch 4 with iOS 5.1.1. Requires a jailbreak and Veency; audio and direct video also need MobileSubstrate and SSH. A separate Mac companion, not an iPod app or Apple’s iPhone Mirroring. Your apps and their data stay in place.
