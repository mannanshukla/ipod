Your iPod.
On your Mac.

A little screen with a bigger home. Tap, swipe, and type from your Mac. A thin frame keeps the screen in focus, with a slim glass bar for Home, Lock, screenshots, audio, and settings.

Download for MacView on GitHub ›

Apple Silicon · macOS 13+ · Experimental, not notarized.
Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later.

Plug in. Bring it over.

Connect your jailbroken iPod by USB, enable Veency, and choose the device in Connection settings. Accept its connection request, then click to tap, drag to swipe, or use your Mac keyboard. Home, Lock, and Screenshot are a click away.

A familiar face.

The original photographic iPod body and classic iOS Home screen, paired with a polished mirror and reflection. A small, always-visible glass bar keeps the controls readable without crowding your apps. The rounded-square Home button feels right at home.

Hear it here, too.

The optional device helpers bring iPod audio to your Mac. An experimental direct screen stream is also available at 320 × 480; Veency still handles input. Audio and video are not synchronized by timestamps, and the iPod speaker remains active.

Made for a classic.

Tested on iPod touch 4 with iOS 5.1.1. A jailbreak and Veency are required; audio and direct video also need MobileSubstrate and SSH for helper installation. This is a separate Mac companion, not an iPod app or Apple’s iPhone Mirroring service. Your existing apps and their saved data stay in place.
